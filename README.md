# ALSmiR

**A curated evidence resource for ALS-associated microRNAs for biomarker discovery.**

ALSmiR is a publicly available, manually curated database of PCR-based microRNA (miRNA) expression findings from human biological fluids and their components. It includes original, peer-reviewed studies comparing patients with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) with healthy controls. Each finding is linked to its source publication, analyzed cohort or subgroup, biospecimen, and reported clinical and methodological information.

🌐 **Website:** https://alsmir.dokhlab.org
📄 **Preprint:** https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.16.759728

## Database content

Release **V08** contains:

| | |
|---|---|
| Publications | 50 |
| Patient cohorts | 78 |
| miRNA expression results | 1,621 |
| Distinct miRNAs | 816 |
| Biospecimens (e.g. serum, plasma, CSF) | 13 |

Each expression record includes the miRNA name, reported expression outcome relative to healthy controls, ALS type or subgroup, numbers of patients and controls analyzed, assay method, and biospecimen. Expression outcomes include increased expression, decreased expression, and no significant difference. Cohort records provide additional clinical and demographic information, including diagnostic criteria, site of onset, sex, and age, where reported.

Reported miRNA names are retained unless a correction is supported by author confirmation. Links to [miRBase](https://www.mirbase.org) release 23.1 are provided only for exact name matches. Names without an exact match remain available without a miRBase link.

## Using the website

- **Evidence table:** Search and filter expression records and download selected records with their cohort and publication information as CSV files.
- **miRNAs:** Explore the curated findings associated with each miRNA.
- **Tissue Explorer:** View counts of records reporting increased expression, decreased expression, or no significant difference for each miRNA–biospecimen combination.
- **Statistics:** Explore publication patterns, cohort characteristics, and reported expression outcomes.
- **Submit:** Submit published studies for curator review. Submissions are checked against the source publications before inclusion.

## How to cite

If you use ALSmiR, please cite:

> Amorós, M. A., Ekambaram, S., Iskandar, R. M., Simmons, Z., & Dokholyan, N. V. (2026). ALSmiR: a curated evidence resource for ALS-associated microRNAs for biomarker discovery. *bioRxiv*. https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.16.759728

Please also cite the original publications associated with the records used in your work. Each record links to its source.
